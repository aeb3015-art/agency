MPRP CRM, Client Portal & Partner Platform

How the platform works, screen by screen. Start with the system map, then switch roles to see what each user sees. Orange notes mark the approval gates and privacy rules that drive the design. All names and figures are made-up examples.

Data flow (permission-checked)Client publication, after company approvalConsent-filtered export to marketing
One record

Every portal reads the same client and case records. Access is decided on the server per role, case, field and document.

Gate

Requests from the Licensed Agent, notary or bank go to an internal review queue first. Default visibility is internal only.

Separation

Marketing sees only contacts with marketing consent. Case, financial and medical data never enter segments.

No government API

Agency submissions happen through the Licensed Agent. The platform records references and milestones; it does not submit to Residency Malta.

Client lifecycle: from enquiry to ongoing compliance

Parallel workstreams

Property, banking and family documents run alongside the application. Stages are operational, not a fixed legal sequence.

Attribution kept

The website, landing page and UTM of the first enquiry stay attached to the client forever, for revenue reporting.

Who decides

Eligibility, due diligence and approval are decided by the Agency. The platform tracks; it does not predict outcomes.
